Agent Licensing Online

The national platform that recruits, trains and licenses insurance agents in Bangladesh, built to the 2021 agent regulations and run until the contract completed in June 2026.

The problem

The Insurance Agent (Recruitment, Registration and Licence) Regulations 2021 set out how agents must be recruited, trained and licensed. In practice the work was slow and paper-heavy, split between the regulator, dozens of insurance companies and the training institutes. A licence took about two weeks.

The process we put online

Four parties, one flow, separate tracks for life and non-life insurers. Each step has a single owner and the platform enforces the regulation, so the regulator reviews complete batches instead of chasing missing papers.

What the platform covers

  • Recruitment: insurers issue time-limited access numbers by SMS, candidates apply online, and approval generates the agent code and appointment letter.
  • Training: institutes run batches and issue certificates, which the licence application then picks up.
  • Licensing and renewal: Form-3 and Form-5 applications in batches, a three-step review at IDRA, generated licences, and a late-fee calculator that follows the regulation.
  • Agent register: the national licensed-agent register, agent history, release letters for agents moving between insurers, and NID verification with the Election Commission.
  • Reporting: summaries by company and training status, eKYC results, and policies sold per agent, shared with the regulator’s policy information system.

My part

  • Turned the regulation into requirements and acceptance criteria with IDRA, and owned that relationship from first build to completion.
  • Led the architecture and delivery: a bilingual Bangla and English web app, an API, a report service for licences and certificates, and an SMS service with per-insurer sender names.
  • Coordinated the rollout across insurance companies and training institutes, each with different levels of digital readiness.

How we delivered

Five years of steady releases, with nearly 4,000 commits across the web, API, report and SMS services. Releases moved through GitLab with manual promotion gates, rolling updates and automatic rollback. API and report services had integration test suites. In the final year we added Prometheus, Grafana and Loki monitoring with business-level alerts, such as a rising NID-verification rejection rate, sent to the team’s chat.
